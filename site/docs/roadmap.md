# Roadmap and known limits

This is a working pilot, not a claim that every original MVP acceptance criterion is complete. The initial experience is implemented; some direct spatial/life evidence and device verification remain incomplete.

## Current foundation

- Two regions, six map anchors, twelve scene records, five original-photo derivatives, six generated life images, and one fictional pedestrian sprite.
- Left-hand scenario-year selection for 2015–2019 and right-hand zoom/detail controls.
- Mapped isometric street rendering, clickable people, contextual prepared thoughts, and photo/information cards for buildings and documented places.
- Element-level evidence, regional/time qualifications, source rights records, and persistent reconstruction labels.
- Optional user-triggered Nano rephrasing with unsupported/error fallback.
- URL/history navigation, image-load retention, reduced motion, and place-list fallback.

## Next priority: street simulation

The user's accepted priority is people movement, building presentation, and click behavior. NASA / OSM remains the selected provider path for now.

1. Validate native Nano and touch interaction on supported physical devices. Preserve the same fictional-thought/evidence boundaries.
2. Improve pedestrian spacing, route variety, visible selection, and interaction stability while keeping positions tied to reviewed map lines. More realistic movement must not imply observed population counts or schedules.
3. Improve building presentation only where footprint, height, facade, or period evidence supports it. Keep illustrative attributes distinguishable from mapped records.
4. Review information-panel usability at constrained widths and during repeated map interactions.

## Evidence work before stronger historical claims

- Obtain reusable, dated Aoji ground/industrial/residential references and better-reviewed spatial matches. Do not fill missing direct evidence with placeholder scenes and call that verified coverage.
- Review Haksong geometry gaps and distinguish a district/station anchor from a verified individual building.
- Review time-specific changes before introducing different annual worlds or implying a verified event in a resident thought.
- Preserve exact prompts and richer production/review records for newly generated assets. Existing retained briefs cannot recover missing exact original prompts.

## Optional later work

- A domain-restricted MapTiler key and imagery-date review, if the provider choice changes.
- A separately deployed Gemma runtime, if browser coverage or editorial control justifies its operational cost.
- More regions/places only after evidence and reuse review.
- Free movement in 3D, video, live scene generation, separately documented eras, and recorded public-figure events require a new scoped implementation and evidence review.

These are future options, not current commitments or implemented features.
